How it started

Deodar opened in 2024 in what used to be a tailor's shop, one floor up from the general store. Two cousins, one second hand espresso machine from a closed down hotel in Shimla, and six tables.

The name comes from the deodar trees on the ridge behind the building. On a clear morning you can see them from the window seats, which is most of the reason people fight over the window seats.

02

How it is going

Fifteen tables now, a proper kitchen, and a regular crowd of students from the university down the road. Half the menu exists because someone asked for it enough times.

We still grind every cup to order, and we still burn one batch of cookies about once a month. Some things you fix, some things become tradition.

Four of them, that is all
