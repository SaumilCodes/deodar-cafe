Small print, asked often

Can we bring a cake?

Yes. No cutting charge, but we will ask for a slice. Candles are fine, sparklers are not, the ceiling is wood.

Do you take large groups?

Up to twelve if you book a day ahead. Beyond that the room stops being fun for everyone else.

Is there power at the tables?

At six of them. Mention it in the notes if you are coming to work and we will keep one aside.

What if we are late?

We hold the table for twenty minutes. After that it goes to whoever has been eyeing it from the stairs.
