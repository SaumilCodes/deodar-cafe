Why people climb the stairs

The coffee

Beans from a small roaster in Chikmagalur, ground when you order. Nothing sits in a pot going stale.

The food

Maggi done properly, steamed momos, grilled sandwiches and one very good brownie. Kitchen runs till ten.

The room

A wood stove in winter, board games on the shelf and wifi that actually works. Laptops welcome on weekdays.
