Finding us

Above the general store

First floor above the general store on the Waknaghat main road, two minutes from the university gate. Look for the orange door and the smell of coffee.

Buses from Solan and Shimla stop right outside. There is no lift, and the stairs are steep enough that the chai feels earned.
